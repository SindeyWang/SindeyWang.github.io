Privacy & cookies隐私与 Cookie

Your choices.由你选择。

The default configuration uses necessary storage only; optional analytics and marketing are disabled. Open Cookie settings to see the current configuration and change your choices.默认配置仅使用必要存储，可选的分析与营销均关闭。打开 Cookie 设置可查看当前启用状态并更改选择。

Necessary storage必要存储

This portfolio stores your selected language in kw_lang and your consent choice in kw_consent in your browser’s local storage. A temporary kw_consent_probe checks whether storage works, then is removed. These entries are not advertising identifiers and are not sent to analytics by this module. Language storage lasts until you clear or replace it. Consent is versioned and valid for 180 days (approximately six months); after expiry a new choice is required. An expired record may remain on your device until it is replaced or cleared.本站在浏览器本地存储中使用 kw_lang 记住语言、使用 kw_consent 记住同意选择。临时的 kw_consent_probe 用于检查存储可用性，随后即删除。这些条目不是广告标识，本模块不会将其发送给分析服务。语言设置保留至你清除或替换。同意记录包含版本，有效期为 180 天（约六个月）；到期后需重新选择。失效记录可能保留在设备上，直到被替换或清除。

Without optional consent, no GTM container, GA4 tag or LinkedIn advertising tag is requested by this module, including cookieless analytics pings. If browser storage fails or a choice is malformed, expired or unsupported, optional tracking stays off. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals override optional consent.未经可选同意，本模块不请求 GTM 容器、GA4 标签或 LinkedIn 广告标签，也不发送无 Cookie 分析请求。浏览器存储失败，或选择记录损坏、过期、版本不兼容时，可选追踪保持关闭。浏览器 Do Not Track 与 Global Privacy Control 隐私信号优先于可选同意。

Optional analytics and marketing可选分析与营销

If configured and enabled in future, Google Tag Manager (GTM) may load only after you explicitly allow analytics or marketing. Analytics consent may permit Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to measure allowlisted page visits, résumé downloads, contact-link kinds, language changes, project categories and a one-time 75% scroll-depth event. Marketing consent is separate and may permit the LinkedIn Insight Tag, managed through GTM. Accepting one category does not accept the other; both start unchecked in customization. Reject all, Accept all and Customize have equal prominence.如未来完成配置并启用，Google Tag Manager（GTM）仅在你明确允许分析或营销后加载。分析同意可允许 Google Analytics 4（GA4）统计白名单页面访问、简历下载、联系链接类型、语言切换、项目类别和每页一次的 75% 滚动深度事件。营销同意独立，可允许由 GTM 管理的 LinkedIn Insight Tag。允许某一类别不代表允许另一类别；初次自定义时两项均不预选。「全部拒绝」「全部接受」「自定义」具有同等显著性。

The module sends only fixed page paths and titles, with query strings and fragments removed; same-site referrers are reduced to allowlisted paths and other referrers are omitted. It does not send email addresses, contact-link destinations, free text, form contents or arbitrary event attributes. Provider tags can receive technical data such as IP address and browser information once permitted. Their actual cookie use, data retention, international processing and consent gates depend on the published provider configuration and must be verified before activation; 180 days describes this site’s consent choice, not provider data retention.本模块仅发送固定页面路径和标题，去除查询参数与片段；站内来源页仅保留白名单路径，其他来源不传递。本模块不发送邮箱地址、联系链接目标、自由文本、表单内容或任意事件属性。允许加载后，服务商标签可能接收 IP 地址、浏览器信息等技术数据。其实际 Cookie 使用、数据保留、跨境处理和同意门控取决于发布的服务配置，启用前必须核验；180 天指本站同意选择的有效期，不代表服务商的数据保留期限。

Provider information: Google Privacy Policy and LinkedIn Privacy Policy.服务商说明：Google 隐私政策与 LinkedIn 隐私政策。

Changing or withdrawing consent更改或撤回同意

Use Cookie settings in the footer at any time. Closing the settings dialog does not grant consent. Withdrawing a category after tags have loaded sends a denied consent update, attempts to remove accessible Google analytics cookies (_ga, _gid, _gat and their supported variants), and reloads the same allowlisted page without its query or fragment to stop loaded tags. This does not undo data already sent. The module cannot remove third-party, HttpOnly or partitioned cookies; use your browser’s controls and the provider’s privacy tools for those.你可随时使用页脚的 Cookie 设置。关闭设置窗口不代表同意。在标签加载后撤回任一类别，将发送拒绝同意更新、尝试删除可访问的 Google 分析 Cookie（_ga、_gid、_gat 及支持的变体），并重新加载同一白名单页面，去除查询参数与片段，以停止已加载标签。这不会撤回已经发送的数据。本模块无法删除第三方、HttpOnly 或分区 Cookie；请使用浏览器设置及服务商隐私工具处理。

Hosting and contact托管与联系

Normal page and asset requests still reach the hosting provider (GitHub Pages), which may process technical request information independently of this optional analytics module. External links and email services operate under their own policies. If you email Kongxin Wang, your message and address are handled by the email service; they are not analytics events. For questions about this site or your choices, use the contact page.正常页面和资源请求仍会到达托管服务 GitHub Pages；托管服务可能独立于可选分析模块处理请求技术信息。外部链接与邮件服务适用各自政策。若给王孔鑫发送邮件，邮件内容和地址由邮件服务处理，不属于分析事件。如对本站或隐私选择有疑问，请使用联系页面。
